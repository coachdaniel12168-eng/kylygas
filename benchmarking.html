Live check: 117 business websites, 17 September 2026

How many business websites are actually ready for AI search?

The 17 September 2026 re-check covered 117 live business websites. 61 of them — 52% — served no llms.txt file, 46 (39%) had no structured data on the homepage, and 16 of the 104 readable robots.txt files (15%) disallowed AI crawlers including GPTBot and PerplexityBot. Most sites are not yet AI-readable.
